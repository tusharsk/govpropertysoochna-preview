Before you join.

What does Rs 9 include?

One region's updates for a month after Early Access. Choose any region; each region is a separate subscription.

How do I get the updates?

Choose your region and join its Telegram channel. Read the Monday bulletin and follow alerts for new openings and deadline changes.

Is Early Access really free?

Yes, for the first 100 early members. No payment now. The regular price is Rs 9/month per region.

Are you a government service?

No. We are an independent information service. We do not sell property or process applications. Use the official authority portal to apply.
